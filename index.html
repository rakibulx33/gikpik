    What we would build

      From idea to live system

      We do not promise the impossible. We check feasibility first, then propose the right solution. Scope is agreed in writing before we build: what is in, what is out, who pays for third-party services, how many revisions you get and what done means. Payment follows milestones.

      Every feasibility check ends in one of three answers

      • Possible
      • Conditional
      • Not viable

      1. Discovery

        We learn the problem, the people who use it, the data, the budget, the deadline and any examples you have.

        You get a requirement summary.

      2. Feasibility

        We check integrations, APIs, platform rules, security and technical risk.

        You get a clear verdict: possible, conditional or not viable.

      3. Scope and quote

        Features, timeline, milestones and price, written down before work starts.

        You get a proposal and an agreement.

      4. Prototype

        A small demo of the most important part of the system.

        You get a demo and a review meeting.

      5. Build and test

        Development, quality checks, regression tests, access control and bug fixing.

        You get a tested deliverable.

      6. Deploy and support

        Hosting and setup, training, handover and the support period we agreed.

        You get a live system and its documentation.

      AI-assisted, human-accountable

      We use AI tools to move faster on drafts, boilerplate and prototypes. A person reviews every requirement, data model, login, payment flow and release. Five gates stand between an idea and your customers.

      1. Scope

        Acceptance criteria, integration limits, estimate and risks in writing.

      2. Build

        Version control, code review, managed dependencies and secrets.

      3. QA

        Happy path and error path, user roles, input validation, backup and restore.

      4. Release

        Staging approval, production checklist, monitoring and a rollback plan.

      5. Handover

        Admin access, deploy notes, invoice, support period and ownership.
